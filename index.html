About Me

I'm a Business student at the University of Utah with a focus on entrepreneurship — I like the process of turning an idea into something real, from the first sketch of a product to a business that actually runs. I'm the founder of a car detailing business, which has taught me as much about operations as any class has. That hands-on experience shapes how I think about AI: I'm most interested in its ability to make processes more cost-effective and efficient, so a business can run leaner without cutting corners. Outside of work, I like building things in a more literal sense too — I spend my free time working on cars and building things out of wood. Whatever the project, I'm happiest when I'm creating something tangible.
